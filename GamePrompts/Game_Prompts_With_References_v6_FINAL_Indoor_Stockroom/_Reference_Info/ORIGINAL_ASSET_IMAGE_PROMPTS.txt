SHEET TITLE: "TICKETS". Design thin rectangular collectible paper tickets with two simple side notches and a stub separator PRINTED on the surface. Top row: one regular cyan/cream ticket large front view and matching back view, clean original graphic design using large simple stars, short serial digits, rectangular silver scratch panel; no currency values, betting or casino imagery. Bottom left: a small fan of six variants cyan, coral, violet, mint, orange, blue, SAME geometry. Bottom right: large golden ticket front and a thin three-quarter view; flat gold foil, large flat printed star, dark gold border, no embossed ornaments or thickness like a metal slab. Include tiny gold corner peeking from underneath three regular tickets as a visibility example. Labels exactly "Regular • Front", "Regular • Back", "Color Variants", "Golden Ticket", "Exposed Corner". Printed details must read as textures on extremely simple planar meshes, 12–40 triangle intent for regular, 12–80 for gold. No thick framing, no extruded lettering.

TICKET FINAL REFINEMENT
Edit this ticket reference sheet. Preserve layout, labels, colors, printed stars and serials, scratch panels and ticket outline. Make EVERY ticket paper-thin, like a flat card, especially the gold ticket, fan and exposed-corner inset. Remove ALL raised borders, inset bevels, extruded stars, embossed printing and slab-like thickness. Gold border and stars are flat printed dark-gold ink on flat gold-foil paper, with no bevel shadows. Edge should be almost invisible. These will be ultra-low-poly planes with texture artwork. Keep clean off-white reference sheet and readable labels. Do not change anything else.
