DECIDED (2026-11-25)
- Leader walks out of the circle or disconnects before travel: the party is disbanded and every member is removed from it.
- The 30-second auto-start countdown RESTARTS from 30 whenever a member joins or the leader changes privacy or size.
- The Lobby place has 6 party circles.
- After the golden ticket is found, players may keep collecting and exchanging the leftover ordinary tickets while the LEAVE / RETURN TO LOBBY options are shown. No new gold and no refill.
- Choosing LEAVE or RETURN TO LOBBY auto-exchanges the unexchanged bag once at the fixed value. A plain disconnect still loses an unexchanged bag.
- A disconnected player cannot rejoin the party's gameplay server; they land in the Lobby.
