- Ride-on users dismount onto safe pit-floor clearance, return the machine to the runtime equipment pad, and climb as a character. Tools are owned inventory, not machines that must be carried up a ladder.

CURRENT CONTRACT (v6 + owner changes, updated 2026-11-25) — applies to every phase
This replaces the earlier "V6 FINAL CONTRACT" wherever the two differ. The change log is in GamePrompts/OWNER_CHANGES.md.

PLACES
TWO Roblox places in one universe (10779008507).
LOBBY place = the start place "Lottery-Game" (id 126779117472575), server size 16. It is the social hub where parties form. It has no shops or economy, and it never claims or saves player profiles.
GAMEPLAY place = "gameplay" (id 81218982278176), server size 4. Each party gets one reserved (private) server. Players who arrive any other way are sent back to the Lobby.

GAMEPLAY MAP
ONE continuous indoor map per gameplay server: the back stockroom of a ticket store. Solid perimeter walls and a complete high roof enclose BOTH zones, which adjoin directly with open internal access and no divider:
- the smaller in-map lobby on the LEFT, with spawn and the TOOLS, UPGRADES, EXCHANGE and INSPECT counters
- the much larger ticket pit on the RIGHT

Dimensions (studs; adjustable after playtests):
- Pit opening 100 x 160 (X 0..100, Z -80..80), flat structural bottom 40.8 below the rim (1.7x the original 24).
- In-map lobby 120 x 120 (X -130..-10, Z -60..60).
- Rim walkway 10 wide.
- Roof underside 28 above the rim.
- Two full-height yellow ladders on the lobby-facing (west) pit wall, at Z -24 and +24, are the normal way back up.

PARTIES (Lobby place)
- Walking into a free party circle creates a party, and that player becomes the leader.
- The leader picks FRIENDS (only the leader's Roblox friends may join) or PUBLIC (anyone may join), and a max size of 1, 2, 3 or 4. The leader can change both while waiting.
- Others join by walking into the circle if the privacy setting allows it and there is room.
- Only the leader sees the START button. A 30-second countdown starts when the party is created and restarts from 30 whenever a member joins or the leader changes a setting; if it reaches 0, the party teleports automatically.
- The whole party travels together to a fresh reserved gameplay server.

HUNT
- ONE shared ticket pile per gameplay server. Each player who joins the server adds a batch of tickets; leaving never removes tickets.
- One hidden golden ticket per hunt.
- There is NO Return-to-Lobby button during the hunt; to go back early, a player leaves the game.
- When the golden ticket is found, every player in that server gets exactly two options: LEAVE (exit the game) or RETURN TO LOBBY (teleport to the Lobby place). These options appear ONLY after the golden ticket is found.

SAVING
- Save Money, OwnedToolIds and UpgradeLevels per player. Only the Gameplay place owns and writes profiles (session-locked).
- Never persist pit state.
- Bag, inspection, equipped selection, vehicle transforms and discovery counts are session-only.
- Schema, lock and purchase-receipt metadata may be persisted for reliability.

MONEY AND PURCHASES
- Earn money by fixed-value ticket exchange, or buy fixed Money packs with Robux.
- Each tool and permanent upgrade has an earned-money route and an optional direct Robux route to the same benefit. Robux purchases are fulfilled in the Gameplay place.
- The Lobby place must still set ProcessReceipt and answer NotProcessedYet, so no purchase is ever auto-acknowledged there.
- Product IDs, prices, amounts and balance are owner configuration. Never invent IDs or enable an unconfigured paid button.

GOLDEN REWARD
The golden-ticket finder receives a special reward in the final game. The exact reward is deliberately TBD. Keep a disabled, server-only finder-reward hook with honest development feedback.
