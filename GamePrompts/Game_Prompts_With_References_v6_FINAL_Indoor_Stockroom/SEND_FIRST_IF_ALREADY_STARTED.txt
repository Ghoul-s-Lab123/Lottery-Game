DESIGN UPDATE — TWO PLACES (2026-11-25)

PRESERVE AND MIGRATE
- Preserve valid work and migrate to TWO places in one universe.
- LOBBY (start place, 16) is the party hub.
- GAMEPLAY (4, reserved server per party) is the enclosed ticket-store stockroom: in-map lobby with shops on the LEFT, big ticket pit on the RIGHT (100 x 160, 40.8 deep), solid walls and a complete roof over both, and two full-height ladders.
- Work built in the start place before this change (data layer, configuration, blockout) must be moved into the Gameplay place. The start place then becomes the Lobby hub.
- Do not delete or unpublish places.

KEEP
- Ten Blender tools, shared authoritative ticket layers, fixed exchange, inspection and working validation.
- The ticket/tool sheets and asset budgets.

SAVING
- Persist Money, OwnedToolIds and UpgradeLevels, owned and written only by the Gameplay place.
- Preserve validated legacy cash, tools and upgrades; initialize only missing levels.
- Never overwrite old progress on a failed load.
- Test migration on copied data. Do not destructively purge records.

PARTIES
- Circle-based parties: FRIENDS or PUBLIC, max size 1–4.
- Leader-only START, plus a 30-second auto-start from party creation.
- The whole party teleports to a fresh reserved gameplay server.

HUNT
- Each joining player adds tickets; leaving removes none.
- No Return-to-Lobby during the hunt. After the golden ticket is found: LEAVE or RETURN TO LOBBY.

PURCHASES
- Optional fixed Robux money packs, and direct Robux alternatives for tools and upgrades; earned-money purchases remain available.
- Both routes use the same server grant logic, fulfilled in the Gameplay place. The Lobby's ProcessReceipt answers NotProcessedYet.
- Product IDs, prices and benefits must be configured and tested before paid buttons are enabled.
- Persist purchase-receipt metadata to avoid duplicate grants, but never save pit state.

GOLDEN TICKET
- The finder will receive a special reward; the exact prize is TBD.
- Finder-only reward hook, disabled in development.
- No invented prize, paid gold boost or future redemption promise.

SESSION-ONLY
- Pit layout, removed tickets, depth, gold location and progress are never persistent.
- Ordinary bags and inspection state remain session-only.

REPORT
- Read all affected phases, then make the smallest consistent migration.
- Report preserved systems, implemented changes, test evidence and pending configuration.
- No live purchases, publication or destructive data actions are requested.
