PARTY CIRCLES (Lobby place)
Six clearly marked party circles on the Lobby floor (owner decision). Each circle holds at most one party.
- CREATE: a player who walks into an empty circle creates a party and becomes its leader.
- LEADER SETTINGS (leader-only UI):
  - Privacy: FRIENDS = only the leader's Roblox friends may join. PUBLIC = anyone may join.
  - Max size: 1, 2, 3 or 4.
  - The leader can change both at any time before travel.
  - Max size cannot be set below the current member count.
- JOIN: another player joins by walking into the circle when:
  - the privacy allows it (FRIENDS: checked server-side with Players:IsFriendsWith / Player:IsFriendsWithAsync, cached, failure = not allowed), and
  - the party has room.
  Players who may not join see a short reason ("Friends only" / "Party full") and are not added.
- LEAVE: a member leaves the party by walking out of the circle, or by disconnecting.
- START: only the leader sees and can press START.
- AUTO-START: a 30-second countdown starts the moment the leader creates the party and RESTARTS from 30 whenever a member joins or the leader changes privacy or size (owner decision). It is shown to the whole party. At 0, if the leader has not pressed START, the party teleports automatically. Pressing START teleports immediately.
- After travel begins, the party is locked: no joins, no settings changes, and the circle frees up once everyone has left.
- Server authority: the client sends only intents (create/join happen by server-side zone detection; settings, start). The server validates leader identity, ranges and state, and rate-limits remotes. Party state is runtime-only and never saved.

TRAVEL
- On START or timeout, the server reserves a NEW Gameplay server for the party (TeleportService:ReserveServerAsync / ReserveServer) and teleports all current members together with TeleportService:TeleportAsync and TeleportOptions.ReservedServerAccessCode.
- Pass party info in teleport data. Treat client-visible teleport data as untrusted; the Gameplay server only uses it for display and grouping.
- Handle TeleportInitFailed: retry with backoff. Members who still fail stay in the Lobby with an honest message, and the party is released.
- Gameplay place:
  - A player who arrives outside a reserved server (direct join, follow, or game.PrivateServerId == "") is teleported to the Lobby.
  - Reserved gameplay servers are only reachable through the party teleport.
- A disconnected player cannot rejoin their party's reserved server. On rejoin they land in the Lobby. (Owner decision.)

POST-GOLD OPTIONS (Gameplay place)
- There is NO Return-to-Lobby button during the hunt. To go back early, a player leaves the game.
- When the golden ticket is found (phase 05 resolution), every player in that server sees exactly two options:
  - LEAVE: the server removes the player from the game with a friendly message.
  - RETURN TO LOBBY: teleport to the Lobby place (any public Lobby server).
- These options appear ONLY after the golden ticket is found. Server-validate that the hunt is resolved before acting on either.
- Before either action, the server auto-exchanges the player's unexchanged bag at the normal fixed value, exactly once (owner decision), then acts.
- Saved progress is written before or during the teleport by the normal leave save. RETURN TO LOBBY must not depend on that save completing first.

PARTIES (Lobby place)
- Walking into a free party circle creates a party, and that player becomes the leader.
- The leader picks FRIENDS (only the leader's Roblox friends may join) or PUBLIC (anyone may join), and a max size of 1, 2, 3 or 4. The leader can change both while waiting.
- Others join by walking into the circle if the privacy setting allows it and there is room.
- Only the leader sees the START button. A 30-second countdown starts when the party is created and restarts from 30 whenever a member joins or the leader changes a setting; if it reaches 0, the party teleports automatically.
- The whole party travels together to a fresh reserved gameplay server.

HUNT
- ONE shared ticket pile per gameplay server. Each player who joins the server adds a batch of tickets; leaving never removes tickets.
- One hidden golden ticket per hunt.
- There is NO Return-to-Lobby button during the hunt; to go back early, a player leaves the game.
- When the golden ticket is found, every player in that server gets exactly two options: LEAVE (exit the game) or RETURN TO LOBBY (teleport to the Lobby place). These options appear ONLY after the golden ticket is found.

SAVING
- Save Money, OwnedToolIds and UpgradeLevels per player. Only the Gameplay place owns and writes profiles (session-locked).
- Never persist pit state.
- Bag, inspection, equipped selection, vehicle transforms and discovery counts are session-only.
- Schema, lock and purchase-receipt metadata may be persisted for reliability.

MONEY AND PURCHASES
- Earn money by fixed-value ticket exchange, or buy fixed Money packs with Robux.
- Each tool and permanent upgrade has an earned-money route and an optional direct Robux route to the same benefit. Robux purchases are fulfilled in the Gameplay place.
- The Lobby place must still set ProcessReceipt and answer NotProcessedYet, so no purchase is ever auto-acknowledged there.
- Product IDs, prices, amounts and balance are owner configuration. Never invent IDs or enable an unconfigured paid button.

GOLDEN REWARD
